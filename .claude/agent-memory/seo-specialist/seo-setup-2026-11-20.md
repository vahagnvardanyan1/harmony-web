First full SEO setup, 2026-11-20. Not deployed yet; the owner reviews the diff.

**Before** (old local build, same as live): 0/21 canonicals, 0/21 HTML hreflang, 0/21 og:url/locale,
0 JSON-LD, robots.txt and sitemap.xml 404, 21/21 descriptions under 120 chars, category titles
were just "Rings · harmony", product photos all `alt=""`.

**After** (local build): every seo-check row passes on 21 pages; 3 × 404 URLs return 404 + noindex.

**Copy choices** (why, not what — the strings are in `messages/*.json` `meta.*`):
- EN bangles title says "Bangles & Bracelets" to catch both words without a volume claim — unverified, check in keyword research.
- HY keeps "ոսկյա" to match existing site copy; "ոսկե" may be the more searched form — open question.
- RU uses "ТЦ «Ташир»" (the common way to write the mall in Russian) and gem names from the catalog ("топаз лондон блю").
- Descriptions only name stones that exist in the catalog: diamonds, London blue topaz, smoky quartz, mother-of-pearl.

**Why:** these were written before any keyword research — see [[keywords]] once it exists and rewrite titles to match.
**How to apply:** treat the current titles as a placeholder baseline, not a researched target; don't defend them against research data.
